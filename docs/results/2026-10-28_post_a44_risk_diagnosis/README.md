# Why A44 N harmed more correct A4C runs

This is an exploratory audit of existing A44 source0 outputs, not a rerun, new threshold choice or an efficacy experiment. Contract1037684 preceded the computation; script87a6ffb. Only existing A44 logs/plans/fit and held-out score caches and immutable A31 training-pool metadata were read. No model/pixel forward or forbidden split. Private ids remain on the server; aggregate audit.json is authoritative.

The objective/score derivative and all600 dual recurrences match the specified implementation. N nevertheless failed its final full-fit H<=.01 condition: .01032931/.18114140 and2/35 false promotions, versus C0/.10311976 and0/15. Last-step sampled H=0 in all models did not certify complete-fit feasibility.

In N’s last50 updates,48/50 and43/50 batches have all3 negatives below the hinge;96.7%/95.0% of negative-window occurrences have zero loss gradient. The remaining active negatives produce occasional strong gradients, with the multiplier around8.3/13.5; this is not the same as a persistent soft classification penalty. Negative score-gradient mass is NOT simply smaller than C (the N ratios to positive mass are large), so the audit does not attribute failure merely to insufficient total negative gradient.

Most final fit errors were already unresolved at their last training encounter. Both N fit0 false promotions were last seen positive;30/35 N fit1 errors were last seen positive,5 were last seen<=0 and only2<=-1. Fit1 last encounters are8–230 updates before the end (mean128). N has19 negatives last seen<=-1 that end with a hinge violation, but only2 become positive. Parameter drift after exposure contributes, and cannot alone explain the35 false promotions. No checkpoint selection or extra fit pass was attempted.

Fit rank AUROC is about.996–1.000 but held-out AUROC is only C.82184/.84661 versus N.83173/.84425: no consistent discrimination gain. C/N held-out scores correlate .98645/.97800. Descriptive affine fits N≈.676*C+2.473 and N≈.825*C+3.339; these are associations, not an intervention or a new deployed threshold. N moves correct-A4C score medians from C−11.55/−13.14 to−5.18/−7.39, nearer the zero promotion boundary. Every C promotion is also made by N. N-only44/10 correct-A4C harms include30/8 pure runs and8/5 native runs; the problem is not confined to mixed-boundary artifacts.

The supported explanation has two parts: the fixed finite nonconvex minibatch procedure never enforced the complete empirical constraint, and a more positive decision region transferred without consistently better A5C/A4C ranking. Weak clip labels and familiar-source fit do not establish anatomical separability. This diagnosis does not prove one unique optimization cause or label error. The nonconvex Lagrangian has no automatic final-iterate feasibility guarantee: [Cotter et al.2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v98/cotter19a.html); A44 is not their proxy-Lagrangian algorithm.

This motivates A45’s distinct learning problem: independent correct-A4C visual support reconstruction and separately held-in calibration to veto uncertain promotions, while freezing the A44 proposals. It does not motivate another multiplier/learning-rate/hinge sweep or promise that reconstruction error detects A5C. Reconstruction OOD scores can miss anomalies ([Denouden et al.2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.02765)); A45 tests a bounded version on frozen representations and claims no anatomical or distribution-free guarantee.
