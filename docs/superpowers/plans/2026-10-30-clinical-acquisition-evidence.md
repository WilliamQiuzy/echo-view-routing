> **For agentic workers:** Codex executes inline; one read-only plan reviewer, no helper execution, Claude or competing GPU queue. Use executing-plans and TDD. User authorized independently justified next training and asked why all iteration stopped; no need for another permission handoff within this scope.

**Goal:** Test an independent source of representation supervision: the recorded clinical measurement purpose of a PW acquisition (LVOT versus mitral inflow), without converting it to per-frame anatomy/visibility or using unverified caliper coordinates.

**Architecture:** A single raw B-mode reference belongs to a published acquisition record with exactly one PW stream and explicitly targeted clinical measurement metadata. Its weak target is the acquisition's recorded purpose, not visible LVOT, exact local position, A5C class or A4C absence. Correct clinical-purpose labels versus shuffled labels on the identical new public image cohort can be a matched pretraining test; transfer must still pass the original heldout A5C/A4C complete-system gates. No measurement values, spectral pixels, sampling poses, model-predicted views, text or overlays become inference inputs.

**Tech Stack:** Pinned EchoXFlow metadata/native tissue frames, full published LFS SHA256 archive verification, safe seekable selected-member reads, canonical/independent image decoding. GPU0 and no model until separate actual-data and training cards pass.

## Why this is an untested condition

A43–A48 trained view/repair models, motion, pretrained clinical/valve states and had no safe A5C package; A49 admitted0/109 global caliper-to-sector bindings and trained no representation. A48's PW operator-location proposal was also untrained. A50's initial local-point draft was reviewed but **not executed or committed as a frozen experiment**; its review found source/temporal association still needs stronger proof for a local target. Keep that draft as an unexecuted hypothesis, rather than dropping its gate and pretending it passed.

The present question is deliberately at a weaker, verifiable label unit: a native recording's explicit measurement purpose. Co-membership in the acquisition store establishes the weak acquisition-level target by definition; it does not establish point/frame-time anatomy. A47 compared an already pretrained report/measurement state to ImageNet; it did not train on this paired new clinical-purpose image cohort. A42 used same-session EV9V view ranking, not these new public images or clinical-purpose labels. The substantive added condition is a new image/clinical-supervision source, not another loss on old EV9V windows, a new deployment gate or an anatomy claim. Source-purpose prediction alone is not proof of A5C transfer.
