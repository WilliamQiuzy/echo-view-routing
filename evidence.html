What these examples establish

The first clip has an A5C label for the whole recording, not expert labels for every frame. Its switches show output instability; they are not all confirmed clinical errors. The second video joins labeled recordings. Its reference changes are known by construction and are scored with a ±0.5 s tolerance. It is not a natural continuous scan.

These are selected failure examples, not a random sample. Aggregate counts cover all 800 native test clips and 379 view changes in 120 constructed test streams. No new inference, training or threshold tuning was used to select or display these predictions.

Source: EV9V, bgx666 · CC BY 4.0. Videos are re-encoded in numeric frame order. The constructed stream preserves the original evaluation’s brightness edits. Labels are sampled at 10 Hz; video playback is 30 fps on that same timeline.
